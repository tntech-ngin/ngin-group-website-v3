Our paper **"Learning-Based Reconstruction Attacks on Coordinate-Obfuscated Point Clouds"** has been accepted as a regular paper at the 2nd Workshop on Enhancing Security, Privacy, and Trust in Extended Reality (XR) Systems (XR Security 2026).

Authors: Mohammad Waquas Usmani (University of Massachusetts Amherst), Susmit Shannigrahi (Tennessee Technological University), and Michael Zink (University of Massachusetts Amherst).
