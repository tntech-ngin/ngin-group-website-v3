Tennessee Tech has received a **$900,000 NSF Major Research Instrumentation (MRI) Track 1 award** (Award #2511818) for *"Acquisition of a Multi-accelerator Architecture for Advanced Scalable Computing Research."*

Dr. Susmit Shannigrahi is a co-Principal Investigator on the award, alongside PI Anthony Skjellum and co-PIs Syed Rafay Hasan, Muhammad Ismail, and Amani Altarawneh, all at Tennessee Technological University.

The award period runs from August 1, 2026 through July 31, 2029.
